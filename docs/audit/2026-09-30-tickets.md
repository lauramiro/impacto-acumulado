## T1. Peak MW as labelled fallback in aggregates

Priority: P0. Gap: G1.

User need: a council or journalist reading "10.804,2 MW" assumes it is the total. It omits about 6.2 GWp that the dataset already holds.

Evidence: 135 projects in `projects.csv` have `mw_peak` and no `mw_nominal` (sum 6,159.9 MWp). `db/aggregate/010_municipality_stats.sql`, `030_protected_area_stats.sql`, `040_province_monthly.sql` and `050_province_stats.sql` sum only `mw_nominal`.

Spec:

- Define `mw_best = COALESCE(mw_nominal, mw_peak)` in `projects_for_aggregates` (migration 003 view), keeping the rule that evacuation lines contribute no MW.
- Aggregates carry three figures per cell: `mw_nominal` (unchanged), `mw_best`, and `mw_peak_fallback_count` (projects whose figure is peak).
- Exports: add `mw_best` and `mw_peak_fallback_count` to `municipality_stats`, `protected_area_stats`, `province_stats`, `province_monthly.csv`. Record the change under "Changes to the published data" in `README.md` and "Cambios" on `/datos`.
- Web: MW metric uses `mw_best`. Coverage line becomes "MW declarados en X de N proyectos; en Y se usa la potencia pico". Methodology gains one bullet explaining that peak (MWp) is higher than nominal (MWn) for solar, so the total leans high where the fallback is used.

Acceptance:

- Regional total with fallback is shown and the fallback count is visible next to every MW total.
- `pytest` covers a project with only peak, only nominal, both, and an evacuation line with peak.
- `mw_nominal` columns keep their current meaning for existing data users.

Open question: whether the map metric should default to `mw_best` or offer a nominal-only toggle.

## T2. Headline and rankings: separate refused, add MW per km²

Priority: P0. Gaps: G2, G13.

User need: "how much is accumulating here" means approved plus pending. Refused projects belong in the record but inflate the ranking; Málaga ranks second mostly on 1,458.1 MW refused.

Spec:

- Home dek (`web/src/app/page.tsx`): report two figures, "aprobados o en trámite" (favorable, favorable_condicionada, en_consulta, desconocido) and "denegados" (desfavorable, caducado). Compute `leaders` from the first figure only.
- Decide how "Sin veredicto leído" is grouped and say so in the dek or legend. Recommendation: with approved or pending, labelled.
- Add a metric "MW por km²" to the map and a column to the municipality index, using `area_ha` from `municipalities.geojson` (MW / (area_ha / 100)).
- Municipality page: add "X % del término" for declared hectares (sum hectares / area_ha), labelled with its coverage ("superficie declarada en X de N proyectos").

Acceptance:

- The dek never sums desfavorable with the rest.
- Index sortable by MW per km².
- e2e test checks the dek figures against `province_stats.json`.

## T3. Review queue for over-merged project clusters

Priority: P0. Gap: G3, G4.

User need: every total depends on one document group being one project. Over-merging undercounts projects and MW; users cannot trust a municipality figure if a project page shows several unrelated plants.

Evidence: `/proyecto/34` "Guadame III" has 16 documents, 8 municipalities, a BOE DIA for "Guadame Solar 5" (138.4 MWp), and at least five Marmolejo AAU notices with distinct PP numbers. Clusters of 10, 16, 18 and 22 documents exist. Marchenilla VII has información pública in 2024 after AAU in 2022. Generic names on projects 339, 439, 598, 615.

Spec:

- New command `python -m impacto review-clusters` writes `tmp/review/clusters.csv` (not published) listing groups that match any rule:
  - more than 4 documents;
  - more than one distinct PP number in document titles (regex on `PP. \d+/\d{4}`);
  - distinct MW values differing by more than 10 %;
  - more than one distinct `expediente`;
  - an información pública dated after a verdict document in the same group;
  - canonical name with no proper noun (generic-name list).
- Each row: project id, rule(s) hit, document ids, titles, MW values, municipalities.
- Corrections go into `resolution_overrides` (document_id, group_key, note) as today. Add a short section to `docs/sources.md` describing the review routine.
- Resolve should prefer the name from a BOE DIA title when present over BOJA "proyecto que se cita" notices.
- Add the review count to the weekly workflow summary so new suspicious clusters are visible.

Acceptance:

- Guadame III split into its constituent projects with overrides; project count and MW change are recorded in "Cambios".
- Unit tests for each rule on fixture groups.
- The number of groups flagged before and after review is written in the PR.

Open question: whether a corrección de errores should be allowed to set status (`pipeline/impacto/resolve/status.py`). Recommendation: it may carry the verdict of the document it corrects, never override a later one.

## T4. Status coverage: read verdicts of BOJA AAU notices

Priority: P0. Gap: G5.

User need: 28 % of projects show "Sin veredicto leído", which hides where approvals are happening.

Evidence: 123 projects `desconocido`; 127 of their documents are `aau` with verdict `no_aplica`, typically "se da publicidad al informe vinculante".

Spec:

- Sample 20 of these documents, label them by hand into `pipeline/evaluation/labels/`, and record whether the verdict is stated in the text, in an attached PDF only, or absent.
- If stated in the text: extend the dispositive-sentence rule in `pipeline/impacto/extract/operative.py` to cover the "informe vinculante favorable/desfavorable" wording, re-extract affected documents with a new prompt version.
- If only in the PDF: add a fetch of the linked PDF for these notices and document it in `docs/sources.md`.
- If absent: keep `desconocido` but relabel it in the UI as "Resolución publicada sin veredicto en el boletín" so users do not read it as an extraction failure.

Acceptance: measured share of `desconocido` before and after, and evaluation accuracy for `verdict` re-run on the new labels and published on `/metodologia`.

## T5. Open consultations with objection deadlines

Priority: P1. Gap: G9.

User need: the most time-critical NGO and council task is knowing what is open for objection and until when. Aragon's IDEAragon and the Ecologistas en Acción viewer both lead with this.

Spec:

- Extraction: add optional fields `information_period_days` (int) and `information_period_unit` (`habiles` | `naturales`) read from the notice text, with an evidence span. Do not assume a default period; if the text does not state one, leave it null.
- Resolve or export: for each `informacion_publica` document compute `deadline` from `published_at` and the stated period. Business-day arithmetic needs a holiday calendar (national plus Andalusia); if that is not available, show "plazo: 30 días hábiles desde el 12 de marzo" without a computed date.
- Export `open_consultations.json`: document id, project id, title, municipalities, published_at, period, deadline or null, source URL.
- Web: a section on the home page and on each municipality page, "En información pública", sorted by nearest deadline, each with a link to the gazette notice. Items past the deadline drop off at build time; the weekly rebuild keeps it current, and the page states the build date.

Acceptance:

- Evaluation labels include the period for at least 10 información pública notices; accuracy reported on `/metodologia`.
- A municipality with an open notice shows it above the project list.

Open question: whether to also compute deadlines for BOE notices, where the consultation is usually announced elsewhere (sede electrónica).

## T6. Atom feeds per municipality and Natura site

Priority: P1. Gap: G10.

User need: councils and NGOs need to hear about new filings without checking the site weekly (PlanningAlerts and Global Forest Watch pattern). The site is static, so feeds fit without a backend.

Spec:

- Generate at build: `/feeds/municipio/[ine].xml`, `/feeds/natura/[code].xml`, `/feeds/andalucia.xml`. Each entry is a document (title, date, verdict, doc type, project link, gazette link), newest 50.
- `<link rel="alternate" type="application/atom+xml">` on the matching pages and a visible "Seguir este municipio (RSS)" link with a one-line explanation for people who do not know RSS, pointing to a free feed-to-email service without endorsing one.

Acceptance: feeds validate against the Atom schema in an e2e test; the weekly data commit updates them.

Out of scope: email accounts or subscription storage.

## T7. Project page: conditions, species, sites, evidence, readable documents

Priority: P1. Gap: G11.

User need: an objection cites specific conditions, species and protected areas. The design spec promised these; the extraction already holds them; the export drops them.

Spec:

- Export: new `project_details.json` keyed by project id with, per document: `expediente`, `conditions[] {category, text}`, `species_mentioned[]`, `protected_areas_mentioned[]`, `evidence{field: quote}`, `utm_coordinates[]`. Source: extraction payloads in `pipeline/impacto/aggregate/export.py`.
- Project page (`web/src/app/proyecto/[id]/page.tsx`):
  - Conditions grouped by category, each tagged with its document.
  - Species and protected areas mentioned as lists.
  - Each fact-sheet figure shows its evidence quote on demand (disclosure element).
  - Document list leads with a short readable label (doc type, authority, date) and the full title; the internal id moves to a secondary line.
  - Mark a corrección de errores as such in the document list.
- Size check: keep per-page data loaded at build time only; no client fetch of the full file.

Acceptance: `/proyecto/1` shows conditions and evidence; a11y e2e still passes; `/datos` documents the new file.

## T8. Developer normalisation and developer pages

Priority: P1. Gap: G8.

User need: "who is behind the nine projects here" is a standard journalist and council question; GEM traces ownership to parent companies. Today there are 362 raw developer strings.

Spec:

- Normalise: strip legal forms (S.L., SL, S.L.U., S.A., S.A.U.), punctuation and accents for a `developer_key`; keep the display name as printed.
- Family key: `developer_key` with a trailing number or roman numeral removed (e.g. "Tayant Investment 12" and "... 15" share a family).
- Manual overrides file `pipeline/reference/developer_groups.csv` (family key to group name, optional parent company and source URL) for known groups.
- Export `developers.json` (key, display names, family, group, project ids, MW by status).
- Web: `/promotor/[key]` pages listing projects, municipalities and status; developer name on project and municipality pages links there.

Acceptance: "Enel Green Power España, S.L." and "Enel Green Power España, SL" resolve to one key; tests cover the normalisation rules.

## T9. Possible-splitting flag

Priority: P2. Depends on T8.

User need: NGOs argue fraccionamiento (splitting a plant to stay under a threshold or avoid a joint assessment). The data already shows the pattern and no other platform surfaces it.

Evidence: 75 of 308 projects with MW between 45 and 50 MW; Tayant Investment 12 to 15, four plants of 49.8 MW in Jimena and Castellar; "Arena Green Power Ren N" as 16 companies.

Spec:

- A group of projects is flagged when all hold: same developer family (T8); at least 2 projects; municipalities shared or adjacent (ST_Touches on municipal geometry); each project between 45 and 50 MW, or their sum exceeds 50 MW while each is below; first documents within 24 months.
- Export `splitting_candidates.json` with the rule values for each group.
- Web: a neutral note on project and municipality pages, "Posible fraccionamiento: N proyectos del mismo grupo promotor, cada uno por debajo de 50 MW, suman X MW", linking to the methodology bullet that explains the rule and states that it is a pattern, not a legal finding.

Acceptance: the Tayant group is flagged; unit tests on synthetic groups; methodology text reviewed for neutral wording.

Open question: whether the 50 MW threshold is the right one for every technology and period. Verify in the applicable law before publishing the wording.

## T11. Outcome views: refusal rate and time to decision

Priority: P2. Depends on T3 for reliable durations.

User need: a local group deciding whether to object wants to know how often projects like this are refused and how long decisions take.

Evidence: desfavorable over decided, Cádiz 22 of 78, Málaga 7 of 17, Sevilla 1 of 114; median about 382 days first to last document on multi-document projects.

Spec:

- Province table gains "Tasa de denegación" (desfavorable over desfavorable plus favorable variants) with the counts shown, not only the percentage.
- Same by technology.
- Time to decision: first información pública to first verdict document, per project, only where both exist; show median and count per province.

Acceptance: every rate shows its denominator; rates with fewer than 10 decided projects are shown as counts only.
