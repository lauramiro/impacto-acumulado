# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- Environmental NGOs preparing objections (alegaciones) to renewable projects, who need totals and the gazette documents that prove them.
- Town councils and local platforms deciding whether to oppose a project in their municipality.
- Journalists and researchers covering the renewables build-out in Andalusia.

Their context is desk research: they arrive with a place in mind (a municipality, a Natura 2000 site, a province) and want to know how much has been approved or is pending there, how much sits in sensitive land, and which BOE or BOJA documents back each figure. Many will cite the site in formal writing, so every number must be traceable.

## Product Purpose

Every solar or wind park in Spain is assessed on its own. Impacto Acumulado turns the environmental declarations and authorisations published in the BOE and BOJA into structured data and shows the running total per municipality, per protected area and per province, over time. Success is a user who can answer "what is the cumulative load here, and where is the proof?" in a couple of minutes, and trust the answer enough to cite it.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, sober, civic. It reads like a public record or a statistics-office publication: evidence first, neutral tone, no activist styling, so both sides of a planning dispute can cite it. Spanish-language copy, plain and precise. Uncertainty is stated, never hidden (coverage counts, "sin dato", methodology with measured accuracy).

## Anti-references

- Official government GIS viewers: toolbars and legends everywhere, layer trees, cluttered chrome that makes the task hard to find.
- Anything that styles the data as advocacy (alarm reds, campaign typography) and so undermines neutrality.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over assertion: every total links to the documents it comes from.
2. Say what the data does not cover: coverage and gaps are shown next to the figures, not buried.
3. Place first: the user's municipality, site or province is the entry point; everything else is secondary.
4. Quiet surface, precise detail: calm hierarchy, dense but readable, one accent.
5. Neutral enough to be cited by either side.
